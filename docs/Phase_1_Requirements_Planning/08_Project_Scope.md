# 8. Scope

**Status:** Draft for approval  
**Date:** 2026-11-07  
**Source:** Phase 1 Requirements & Planning v1.0

In scope: multi-stock research, historical news, forward sentiment, charts, reports, authenticated access, 3 free jobs, unlimited active-token access, research history and citations.

Out of scope: brokerage transactions, automated trading, payment processing, guaranteed price predictions and training custom ML models.
