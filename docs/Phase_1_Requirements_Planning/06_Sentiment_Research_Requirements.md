# 6. Forward-looking analysis rules

**Status:** Draft for approval  
**Date:** 2026-11-07  
**Source:** Phase 1 Requirements & Planning v1.0

| Topic | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Horizon | 1, 7, 30, 90 days. |
| Outlook | Bullish, bearish, neutral or mixed; evidence strong/moderate/weak/insufficient. |
| Evidence | Explain favorable catalysts, downside risks, contradictory information and uncertainty. |
| Historical research | Do not use information published after the historical as-of date (no look-ahead bias). |
| Claims | No guaranteed price prediction, causal claims without evidence, or personalized investment advice. |
